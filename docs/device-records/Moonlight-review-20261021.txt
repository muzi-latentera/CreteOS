Findings
1. The recent successful session was measurably less smooth. The 20 October 23:37:38 client session, ending around 21 October 00:08:28, requested 2560x1600 at 120 FPS and 80 Mbps HEVC SDR. Incoming/decoded/rendered rates were 76.58/76.58/76.56 FPS. Network frame loss was 0.00%, jitter drops 0.02%, estimated RTT 1 ms with reported variance 0 ms. Average G1 decoding was 1.29 ms and rendering 1.76 ms. Host processing min/max/average was 4.3/57.0/18.2 ms. The client generally kept up with frames delivered; the evidence points toward host/game/capture delivery rather than sustained network loss as the main limitation in that session. It does not isolate game rendering from capture, scheduling or encoding.
2. The recent -1 error was a control-channel disconnection contemporaneous with clear local Intel WLAN driver failures. Native Windows System log Netwtw14 event 5005 repeatedly reports the AX210 encountered an internal error and failed, around 23:36:09 and 23:36:16 on 20 October. Moonlight logs an unexpected control disconnect and Connection terminated: -1 at elapsed 00:00:16 in the 23:36:02 process. Sunshine logs CLIENT DISCONNECTED at 23:36:13 without restarting. WLAN AutoConfig then records driver-initiated disconnection from Extra at 23:36:27 and successful reconnection at 23:36:28. The client later observes Bosgame offline then online. The timing strongly supports the local adapter/driver failure as the immediate reason that attempt was lost. The underlying driver trigger is not established.
3. The WiFiSmart events show protect-stream and no network-switch request around this failed launch. This was not an Extra-to-Fibre automation transition. Refresh/retry plausibly succeeded after the adapter recovered; there is no evidence that re-pairing was required.

Client findings
At collection the G1 was on Extra at 5 GHz, RSSI -54 dBm, 86% signal and 574 Mbps negotiated link. These are current radio values, not historical throughput measurements.
Installed Intel AX210 Wi-Fi driver version: 23.150.0.4. Moonlight version 6.2.0; AMD client display driver 32.0.31041.1004. The driver has explicit failure evidence; do not infer a GPU decoder failure from the generic -1 code.
Moonlight reports no matching display mode for 120 FPS, uses desktop mode and enables V-sync. This may affect cadence on the 144 Hz panel, but the same warning occurred previously and does not explain the latest low incoming frame rate by itself.
Startup/shutdown decoder queue overflows and OperationCanceledError on teardown should not be confused with repeated mid-game decoder or server crashes. Control stream 10004 messages during orderly shutdown are separate from unexpected -1 termination.
