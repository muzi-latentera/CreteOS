## Artwork and proposed second-SSD layout — 26 October 2026

Read-only inspection requested while discussing Ground Zeroes' tile and adding a second 2 TB SSD to Bosgame. No artwork, game location, mount, filesystem, profile or settings change was performed. Only one NVMe drive is currently visible, the 2 TB YMTC PC41Q. A future second SSD's identity, interface and performance are not yet verified. This entry is a proposed layout, not an installed storage change. No fresh personal archive is needed for this documentation-only work; existing private backups remain their dated snapshots.

The Ground Zeroes library tile is visibly a landscape banner inside a mostly empty portrait card. The existing official Steam `4139357587p.jpg` is actually a PNG at 600x900 whose pixels already contain the horizontal banner and blurred padding; the bad framing is in the supplied asset, rather than proof of an incorrect card aspect-ratio implementation. A proper portrait alternative should replace the grid artwork when selected; hero art is a separate setting. Artwork was inspected through the Windows computer-use skill and the actual host asset; it was not changed.

Bosgame's current Btrfs filesystem reports 1.86 TiB device size, 1.72 TiB used and 77.67 GiB estimated free (df rounds to 96% full). Selected directory totals: Steam steamapps ~853 GiB, Steam common games ~805 GiB, Lutris Games ~207 GiB, Downloads 3.7 GiB. Phantom Pain's live directory is ~26 GiB. These are directory-accounting figures, not a complete physical-space attribution. A slow top-level home du did not produce a complete home breakdown and must not be represented as one.

Btrfs FIEMAP-aware staging inspection: original game-work total 25.13 GiB, exclusive 0; game-gz-work total 26.94 GiB, exclusive 1.88 GiB; rollback tree total 4.25 GiB, exclusive 36 KiB. These sets share extents with other files, so summing their apparent sizes overstates physical consumption and deleting a shared copy does not necessarily free its apparent size. Full mods/staging are not the principal measured library-space consumer. Source: https://btrfs.readthedocs.io/en/stable/Reflink.html and https://btrfs.readthedocs.io/en/stable/btrfs-filesystem.html .

Recommended proposal: retain the OS, existing prefixes, actively modded Phantom Pain and its local staging/rollback workflow on the current SSD; aim for roughly 350–450 GiB free by relocating roughly 300–400 GiB of large ordinary Steam games once the new drive is installed. An illustrative set is Crimson Desert ~142 GiB, DOOM ~69 GiB, Lies of P ~60 GiB, Returnal ~54 GiB and Onimusha ~56 GiB: ~381 GiB apparent total. These are candidates only, not an instruction to move those particular games or a guarantee of immediately reclaimed physical space; snapshots/shared extents must be checked during migration. Leaving 300–400 GB free on the current SSD is sensible for the user's modding and update workspace.

Use the new SSD principally for the bulk game library, while retaining roughly 150–200 GB free for installation/update operations rather than filling it to 100%. Keep it a separately mounted filesystem with a stable mount path, add a Steam library and move Steam games using Steam's storage manager. For this Linux host, a separate Btrfs filesystem is a reasonable default; do not combine both SSDs into one multi-device Btrfs volume merely to gain capacity. Existing same-filesystem reflink mod copies should stay together; cross-filesystem moves cannot preserve that shared storage. Retarget any moved Lutris executable paths deliberately, preserving IDs/prefixes and verifying MoonDeck launch/exit afterward. No files were moved, deleted or formatted in this audit.
